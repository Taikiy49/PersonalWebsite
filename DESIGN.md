# Design direction
Professional studio portfolio inspired by the hierarchy and product storytelling of Linear, clarity of Brittany Chiang, and motion ambition of Bruno Simon. Original implementation; no commercial template or copied assets.
Audience: engineering recruiters and collaborators. Surface mode: Experience, with readable work history.
Palette/runtime source: src/styles/PortfolioSite.css :root. Midnight #080f21, panel #111c32, blue #698fff, mist #edf2ff, muted #a5b1cd, line #293650.
Type: self-hosted Archivo display, Manrope body. Left-aligned oversized hero, generous asymmetric project layouts, restrained navigation. A living terrain field connects geotechnical data to deployed software; its contour lines become ordered signals. Quiet sections support the signature hero rather than repeating it.
Motion: orchestrated hero entrance; canvas terrain movement and pointer response; staggered project line drawing; hover image motion; scroll section reveals; animated navigation indicator; pause control. Reduced-motion disables all decorative motion and renders final readable states. Native scrolling, no scroll hijack or custom cursor.
Content: LinkedIn is authoritative for work. Project graphics are labeled conceptual visualizations, never presented as real screenshots. Original personal photos only. No fictional demos or claims. Contact opens email or LinkedIn.
Responsive: 360px through desktop, focus-visible and skip link, native details disclosures, semantic navigation, static canvas fallback.

Owner update: the supplied seaside suit portrait is the introduction profile avatar; the supplied kimono portrait replaces the old About photograph. Preserve both original colors and square compositions.
